Start here · the core

Three rules. Nothing else for two weeks.

Most systems fail on upkeep, not capture. The core has almost none: one page a day, and a five-minute look back on Sunday. It stays fixed. Everything else is an add-on you choose.

1

Date the page. List today.

One page per day, front side only. Write what you plan to do and work down it. Cross things off with ×.

2

New → next page.

Anything that turns up goes on tomorrow’s page. Today’s list stays closed so it can actually be finished.

3

Dates → tell your phone.

Paper can’t remind you at 2 pm on Thursday. Say it to your phone’s assistant when it comes up.

Moved three times? Do it or drop it.  Sunday: flip back, five minutes.

How they fit

A fixed core. Add-ons only on the second slip.

Start

Tape the core card inside a $1 spiral notebook. Three rules, two weeks.

Carry

Notebook and pen in your pocket. Dates go to the phone by voice.

Slip

Hit the same problem twice? Find it in the Slip Deck and tuck that card in the cover.

Add on

Plug in one add-on that passes the five rules: trigger, place, hook, cost, exit. Unplug it when it stops earning its place.

Where it comes from

Borrowed ideas, cut down to a pocket.

The closed list and tomorrow-by-default come from Mark Forster’s Do It Tomorrow. The marks come from Ryder Carroll’s Bullet Journal. Next actions, Waiting for and Someday come from David Allen’s Getting Things Done. Thirty-minute starts come from Neil Fiore’s The Now Habit. The evening sort (Defer, Act, File, Toss) comes from Andrea Cannavina. Any mistakes in cutting them down are mine.
