When it slips

Moved three times?

Break it into a smaller first step, give it a date in your phone, or put a line through it. A task you keep moving is a decision you haven’t made.

Missed some days?

Don’t fill in the gap. Date today’s page, copy over only what still matters, carry on.

Can’t finish the list?

Three days running means one of these:

  • doing a lot that isn’t on the list
  • agreed to too much (cut commitments)
  • no time left for ordinary life

Stuck on one task?

Write the smallest start: “just find the folder”. Do 30 minutes, then you may stop. Start the next thing before a break.

Suggested add-ons

Back pages start from the back of the notebook. One new add-on at a time.

WaitingYou forgot someone owed you. Who, what, when asked.
NowA goal never reaches a day page. Five at most, each with a next step.
SomedayTomorrow fills with not-this-week things.
LaterDates on paper, not phone. One place only.
HabitOne row of 7 boxes on the Weeks page. Tick as you date the page.

30-min tally when days feel empty. Countdown plan for a deadline. Make your own: write its five rules at the top of its page.

Folding the pocket guide

Print page 1 only, single-sided, at Actual size / 100% (turn off “Fit to page”). The top row prints upside down on purpose.

Steps

  1. Crease it into eighths. Fold in half top to bottom, unfold. Fold in half left to right, unfold. Fold each short edge in to the centre crease, unfold. You now have 8 panels.
  2. Cut the slit. Cut along the dashed line on the centre crease. It runs across the middle two columns only, never to the edge.
  3. Fold it long. Fold the sheet in half along the long centre crease, printed side out, so the cover (“Next Page”) faces you on the bottom-right.
  4. Push the ends together. Hold the two short ends and push them toward each other. The slit opens into a diamond and then a plus shape.
  5. Close the book. Fold the four wings flat around the cover so it reads 1 to 8. Crease the spine hard.

Size

The finished guide is A7 (74 × 105 mm). It fits the back pocket or cover of most pocket notebooks. For an A7 notebook, trim a couple of millimetres off the outside edges.
