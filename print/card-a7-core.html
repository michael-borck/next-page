Next Page core card

Print page 1 only, single-sided, at Actual size / 100% (turn off “Fit to page”). One sheet makes four cards.

Make a card

  1. Cut along the dashed lines. You get four strips, each 148 × 105 mm (A6).
  2. Fold each strip in half along the dotted line, printed sides out. The rule card is the front and the reference is the back.
  3. Glue or tape it inside the front cover. Double-sided tape at the top edge lets you lift it to read the back.

Sizing

The folded card is A7 (74 × 105 mm). If your notebook is also A7, trim 2 to 3 mm off the edges so it sits inside the cover. For an A6 notebook it fits with room to spare. Keep the spare cards for your next notebooks.
